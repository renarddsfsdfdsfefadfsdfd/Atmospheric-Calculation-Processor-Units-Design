Why backside power
• front-side tracks freed for signal: +5.4% routed area
• IR drop 62 → 48 mV at 80 A
• via resistance 1.8 → 0.9 Ω

BSPDN is what makes a 0.75 V, 80 A, 65 W part practical in a two-slot card: the front-side grid alone would have needed 14 metal layers.
